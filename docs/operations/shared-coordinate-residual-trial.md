# Test one common mixture of compatible learned corrections

The preceding decoupled study completed and failed all-region accuracy acceptance.
Lower local-relation training error did not preserve classification quality,
especially for Auckland CNN. The earlier semantic-residual case remains closest
among nine common candidates from five audited balanced-v2 studies, but is still
1.5267 percentage points below the stronger reference in its weakest slot.
All negatives and the increased Harbin CNN public floor of 61.0796% remain.

Before another trial, only the rejected gated study's eighteen exported annual
embeddings and eighteen deterministic evaluation grids were retired. Enumerating
all internal hardlinks identified 48 paths and 36 unique physical files. Actual
free space increased by exactly 31.7112 GiB, to about 151.28 GiB. The eighteen
terminal weight checksums, results, prediction records, sampling and thresholds,
figures and upstream sources remain. Current selected public-reference arrays and
original best model bundles were excluded. Frozen recipes and the old pinned
implementation remain available to rebuild the retired arrays without refitting
readers. Deletion manifests and individual records are retained outside Git.

A new metadata/weight-buffer audit checked all nine region/role pairs. The earlier
semantic-residual output and the new wide decoupled output share the same public
anchor payload, geographic index and exactly equal public normalization buffers.
Both are 64D. Consequently a common convex mixture retains the public anchor and
combines their corrections in compatible coordinates; no learned rotation or PCA
is introduced. This algebra is not evidence that classification will improve.

Two candidates were registered before creating mixed embeddings or scores:

- 85% semantic-residual output plus 15% wide decoupled output.
- 70% semantic-residual output plus 30% wide decoupled output.

One fixed coefficient applies to all regions, source roles, tasks and readers.
The same pair of common source methods supplies native, genuine-public and
degraded controls. Selection of the two source methods used their global
worst-slot ranks, not separate regional or task winners. Mixtures are computed in
float32 and stored in the existing float16 embedding format. There is no new
encoder fit. This is an exploratory composite representation, not evidence that a
single component improved, and not an accepted standalone production exporter.

Check source-payload identities and small numerical cases before exporting all
eighteen arrays. Pin the unchanged validated core/readout implementation and the
prototype script identity. Fit all thirty-six fresh readers and preserve all 240
conditions under the same balanced-v2 cohort, twenty dense support tiles, one
hundred reader updates, both readers and original seeds. Preserve all six strict
AEF/public wins and each region's 0.5 percentage point mean AEF gain requirement.
The public envelope can only increase. Only an eligible common coefficient gets
queries with frozen heads and thresholds; no negative task is removed.

The estimated additional peak is 37 GiB with a 100 GiB reserve. The exporter and
evaluator have not yet been created or launched at registration. A successful
prototype would still need a tested standalone implementation, both fusers' full
source/rebuild closure, complete compute costs, contribution checks and the
remaining paper-task evidence. No Overleaf body edit or goal-completion claim is
made from this preparation.
